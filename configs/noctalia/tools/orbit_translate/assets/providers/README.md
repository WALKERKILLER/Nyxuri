# Provider artwork

The existing SVG/PNG service artwork comes from Pot's `public/logo` assets.

`mymemory.png` is the unmodified 32×32 brand favicon declared by the
[official MyMemory website](https://mymemory.translated.net/), downloaded from
[`/public/img/favicon-32x32.png`](https://mymemory.translated.net/public/img/favicon-32x32.png).
It is used solely to identify the translation service; brand rights remain with
their respective owners. This attribution does not relicense brand artwork.

models.dev provider logos are optional public assets fetched separately at
runtime, not bundled here. Their upstream MIT notice accompanies the local
public-logo cache.
